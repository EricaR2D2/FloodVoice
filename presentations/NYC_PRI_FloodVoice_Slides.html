🚨 The Problem

"During Floods, Vulnerable New Yorkers Are Invisible"

Who is affected:
  • 50,000-100,000 basement apartment residents
  • 15% of NYC aged 65+ (digital divide)
  • Non-English speakers, residents with disabilities
Hurricane Ida (2021):
  • 11 deaths in NYC
  • 10,000+ calls/hour overwhelmed 911
  • Text alerts failed vulnerable populations
Emphasize the human cost. Ida exposed critical gaps in our emergency response infrastructure. The fundamental problem: responders can't distinguish life-threatening from non-urgent when overwhelmed.

🎯 The Core Challenge

Emergency responders cannot distinguish between:

🔴 LIFE-THREATENING

"Trapped in basement, water at my waist"

Requires immediate rescue

🟢 NON-URGENT

"Street is flooded, I'm safe upstairs"

Can wait

Result: Undifferentiated qualitative data → Preventable delays → Loss of life

💡 The Solution: FloodVoice

AI-Powered Community Triage

One-Line Description:
An automated voice-based welfare check system that converts thousands of qualitative phone conversations into a structured, prioritized emergency response dataset in real-time.
FLOOD DETECTED → LIAISONS ALERTED → AI CALLS RESIDENTS → NARRATIVES ANALYZED → TRIAGE DASHBOARD
     ↓                  ↓                     ↓                       ↓                       ↓
FloodNet Sensors → Telegram Alert → Voice AI Welfare → Gemini AI NLP → Priority Queue
  (>4" depth)        to CBO Staff         Check Calls        Sentiment Tags      for Responders

🚀 Three Key Innovations

1. Zero-Friction Access

No app required—works with any phone (landline or cell)

2. Massive Scale

1 liaison can check 500 residents in 60 seconds (vs. 10/hour manually)

3. Narrative-to-Numbers

AI extracts sentiment, urgency, and specific needs from natural speech

🔄 The Transformation

Qualitative → Quantitative

INPUT (Qualitative - Raw Voice):
"I'm okay, but... the water is coming into my basement. It's about ankle-deep now. I moved my things upstairs."
⬇️
OUTPUT (Quantitative - Structured Data): 
{
  "sentiment_score": 4,

  "key_topics": "Basement flooding ankle-deep, relocated belongings",
  "risk_label": "MODERATE"
}

🛠️ Implementation: Voice AI

How spoken words become structured data

Technology Stack:
  • Vapi.ai - Outbound call platform
  • GPT-4o-mini - Conversational AI
  • ElevenLabs - Voice synthesis
  • Real-time transcription
Conversation Flow:

1. SAFETY: "Are you in danger?"
  → If YES: Call 911, report distress

2. INVESTIGATE: "Is there flooding?"
  → "How deep is the water?"

3. CLOSE: "I've updated your status."

🧠 Implementation: NLP Analysis

Google Gemini AI Sentiment Classification

Sentiment Scoring (1-10):
  • 1-3: Calm, safe
  • 4-6: Concerned, mild needs
  • 7-8: Distressed, urgent
  • 9-10: Panic, life-threatening
Fixed Taxonomy (7 Tags):
  • Medical
  • Food/Water
  • Power
  • Evacuation
  • Mental Health
  • Property Damage
  • Safe

Model Fallback Chain: gemini-2.5-flash-lite → gemini-2.5-flash → gemini-2.0-flash-lite → gemini-2.0-flash
