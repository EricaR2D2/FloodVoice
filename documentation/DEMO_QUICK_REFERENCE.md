## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls & Fixes

| Pitfall | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Vapi calls don't trigger** | Stay calm. Say: "Let me show you the pre-recorded version." Switch to backup video. |
| **Dashboard doesn't update** | Refresh page. Say: "WebSocket hiccup. This is why we test." |
| **Telegram alert doesn't appear** | Have a screenshot ready. Say: "Here's what the alert looks like." |
| **Audience asks technical question you don't know** | "Great question. Let me connect you with our technical lead after this." |
| **Someone challenges AI accuracy** | "That's why we have human-in-the-loop. Liaisons verify. AI is a tool, not a replacement." |
| **Someone asks about cost** | "$0.10-0.20 per resident. $50-100 per event for 500 people. Compare to deploying 50 staff." |

## 🎤 Q&A Cheat Sheet

**Q: Language barriers?**  
A: "We capture preferred language. Vapi supports Spanish, Mandarin, etc."

**Q: Consent?**  
A: "Liaisons obtain verbal consent during registration. Residents can opt out anytime."

**Q: AI mistakes?**  
A: "Human-in-the-loop. Liaisons verify. Full audio + transcript available."

**Q: Cost?**  
A: "$0.10-0.20 per resident. $50-100 per event for 500 people."

**Q: Vapi downtime?**  
A: "Fallback to SMS. Cached status. Manual call list. Redundancy built in."

**Q: Privacy?**  
A: "Encrypted. Row-Level Security. HIPAA-aware. Auto-delete after 90 days."

**Q: Integration with 311?**  
A: "Post-MVP feature. Data is structured and exportable via API."
