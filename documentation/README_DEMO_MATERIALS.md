## 🎬 Recommended Demo Scenario

**Scenario B: 2 In Distress (Balanced Drama)**

**Test Residents:**
- 8 safe residents (scores 1-5) - Minor flooding, power outages
- 2 distress residents (scores 8-9) - Trapped, medical emergencies
- 1 unresponsive resident - No answer

**Why this scenario:**
- ✅ Dramatic enough to be compelling
- ✅ Controlled enough to stay on message
- ✅ Shows full range of AI capabilities
- ✅ Triggers Telegram distress alerts (wow factor!)
- ✅ Demonstrates Priority Queue sorting

**Alternative scenarios in `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` if you want more/less drama.**
